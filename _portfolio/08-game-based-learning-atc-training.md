## Research Problem

Air traffic controller training requires trainees to progress from learning aviation rules and procedures to applying them in **dynamic traffic situations under time pressure**.

High-fidelity ATC simulators are essential for advanced training, but they are expensive, require instructor supervision, and provide limited opportunities for trainees to repeatedly practise foundational competencies.

There is therefore an opportunity for **game-based learning** to complement classroom and simulator training by providing accessible environments in which probationary air traffic controllers (PATCOs) can practise core concepts before entering high-fidelity simulation.

## Contributions

Our research develops and evaluates **game-based learning systems for foundational air traffic control training** in collaboration with the Singapore Aviation Academy.

Key contributions include:

- A **Radar Vectoring Game** for practising vectoring, conflict avoidance, and area-control concepts
- A **Runway Sequencing Game** for practising arrival merging, sequencing, and aircraft separation
- A **Ground Traffic Management Game** for practising aircraft movement between gates and runways
- A cloud-based platform that records trainee decisions and supports repeated scenario-based practice
- Deployment with **28 PATCO trainees across 36 scenarios**, generating more than **3,000 gameplay instances**
- Analysis of gameplay data and trainee feedback to refine scenario difficulty, performance measures, and scoring methods
- Progression from prototype development to an operationally tested learning-support platform at **TRL 6**
