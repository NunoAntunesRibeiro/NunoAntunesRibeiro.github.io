## Research Problem

Aircraft turnaround requires many ground-service activities to be completed within a short period, including baggage and cargo handling, catering, cleaning, servicing, and other aircraft-support tasks.

These activities share limited **ground service equipment (GSE), vehicles, and personnel**, and many tasks can only start after preceding activities have been completed. Equipment must also travel through a congested apron environment between successive jobs.

Poor coordination can therefore increase turnaround time, create equipment conflicts, and propagate delays to aircraft departure operations.

## Contributions

Our research develops **integrated resource-allocation, scheduling, and routing models** for aircraft turnaround operations.

Key contributions include:

- Optimization models for assigning **ground vehicles and equipment to turnaround tasks**
- Integrated scheduling and routing approaches that account for **task precedence, service requirements, travel times, and operational deadlines**
- Mesoscopic models that represent the interaction between multiple turnaround processes and equipment movements
- Vehicle-routing and scheduling methods for **baggage and cargo transport operations**
- Integration of data-driven service-time estimates and process information into optimization models
- Development of models suitable for increasingly **automated and autonomous ground-service operations**
